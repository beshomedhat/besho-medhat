Between the

I'm Besho Medhat — a filmmaker, assistant director, and production specialist based in Egypt. My work lives in the space between meticulous planning and spontaneous creativity: keeping sets moving, stories breathing, and teams connected.

I bring both creative vision and operational precision to every production. Beyond film, I carry a background in web development and programming — a rare blend that sharpens my problem-solving and technical adaptability on set.

Who

Not just
another
crew member

I'm still early in my journey, but what I lack in years I make up for in commitment. Every set I've worked on has taught me something new — about people, about process, about how fragile a shoot can be and how much a calm, prepared presence can change that. I don't have all the answers yet, but I know how to listen, adapt, and show up ready to give everything I have to the work.

I also code.

Web development, programming, system thinking — not as a hobby, but as a profession. On set, this means I talk to technical teams in their own language and solve problems before they become problems. It's a rare combination.
